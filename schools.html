01 — context

School in Kivu

Primary lasts six years. Many kids still walk far or learn under tarps when roofs fail.

School is free on paper. Broken roofs, late teacher pay, and long walks still keep kids out.

What kids face

Far walks on mud roads. Crowded rooms. No light at home for homework.

What we fund

Solid classrooms, desks, books, teacher pay, and catch-up classes.

Quick facts

  • Primary cycle6 years
  • LanguagesFrench + local
  • Main barriersDistance, fees, roofs
  • Rainy seasonHigh absenteeism
  • Teacher payOften irregular
  • Our focusRooms, staff, catch-up
